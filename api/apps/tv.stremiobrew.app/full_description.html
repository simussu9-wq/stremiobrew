StremioBrew — an unofficial, Stremio-compatible client for older rooted LG webOS TVs (webOS 3 and later).

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return. Debrid and P2P playback, embedded and external subtitles with auto-sync, skip intro/credits, 11 languages.

Requires a rooted TV with the Homebrew Channel, webOS 3.0 or later on 32-bit ARM; tested on webOS 4.4. Not affiliated with Stremio.

Source: https://github.com/simussu9-wq/stremiobrew
